01RESEARCH NOTE
JANUARY 2024
KATHMANDU UNIVERSITY / COMP 480: E-GOVERNANCE

E-Learning Amidst COVID-19 in Nepal

Ashish Thapa · Rajaram Karki · Shashank Verma

A collaborative course research paper exploring students’ experiences with online learning in Nepal during the COVID-19 pandemic. We gathered 38 survey responses and used the Mann–Whitney U test to compare perceptions across university, residency, and access to technology.

The study looks at flexibility, infrastructure, participation, and the challenges of moving education online.
